Welcome to nap

nap keeps double-entry books on nostr. A ledger, its entries and its reports are signed events on the relays you choose, so the books stay yours and stay verifiable by anyone you share them with.

Connect a signer, then open a ledger or create your first one. Entries you post to it show up here.
